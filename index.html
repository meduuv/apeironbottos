04 / EXPLOITS AND REPORTING

Zero tolerance for economy bugs or exploits.

Using, sharing, or intentionally abusing bugs, vulnerabilities, command loopholes, permission flaws, or unintended behavior in Apeiron is prohibited. This includes economy exploits such as duplicating currency or items, bypassing cooldowns, abusing rewards, or manipulating balances.

!
Found a bug or exploit?

Stop using it and report it privately to an Apeiron developer. Include what happened, how to reproduce it safely, and any relevant evidence. Do not publish the exploit or use it to gain an advantage.

Good-faith reports that help us verify and fix a genuine vulnerability or economy exploit may receive an in-bot reward, bonus currency, perks, or other recognition at the developers' discretion. Rewards are not guaranteed, and reports must not involve harming users, accessing data without permission, or exploiting the issue for personal gain.
